# Version 1.1

For the York inverse, the spectral reconstruction is compared with a separate free-space Green tensor quadrature. Initialized transport from the two chiral inputs gives a nonlinear far-field coefficient for comparison with a separate constraint iteration on the same grid. The specified right inverse in Appendix B extends a nonanalytic plane-wave slice while preserving its interior. In the local reconstruction, the direct band-96 tidal error is 0.20% of the rising-side signal. For a separate smooth band-32 representative, the straight and broken paths differ by $7.17\times10^{-11}$ in $K$.

The computational supplement paragraph gives local and release links, and the disclosure identifies the original and additional computational environments.
